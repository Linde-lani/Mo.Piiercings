About Mo's Piercings

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Mo's Piercings has been serving the community with passion, professionalism, and a commitment to body art excellence. Based in Braamfontein, Johannesburg, we offer piercings, tattoos, tooth gems, and premium jewelry in a welcoming and creative studio space.

Our founder, Mo, has over 15 years of experience in the body art industry. Her vision was to create a safe, welcoming, and creative space where clients could express themselves through body modification.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality body art services in a clean, professional, and inclusive environment while prioritizing client safety, comfort, and satisfaction.

Why Choose Us?

  • Sterile & Safe: We use only sterile, single-use equipment and follow all health regulations
  • Experienced Artists: Our team consists of certified and highly skilled professionals
  • Premium Materials: Only the highest quality jewelry and ink products
  • Custom Designs: Personalized consultations for your unique vision
  • Affordable Pricing: Competitive rates without compromising quality
  • Excellent Aftercare: Detailed instructions and support for proper healing
  • Welcoming Atmosphere: A judgment-free zone for all body art enthusiasts
